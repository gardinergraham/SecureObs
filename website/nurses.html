Core nurse responsibilities

Observation evidence

Record location, presentation, notes and missed-observation reasons against the correct patient.

Clinical records

Use NEWS2, medication chart, patient notes and care plans to support safe decisions.

Shift control

Use incidents, tasks, rota cover and handover summaries to keep ownership clear.

Nurse route

Sign in and confirm ward

Check the ward, shift and staff identity before entering clinical information.

Record general observations

Select the patient, location and presentation, add notes if needed and save the check.

Use clinical modules

Complete NEWS2, food/fluid, medication administration, enhanced observations and security-related tasks where enabled.

Add notes and care plans

Record signed patient notes and structured care plans with needs, risks, goals, interventions and review dates.

Prepare handover

Review the generated shift summary, add clinical context and sign handover when ready.

Handover remembers the important things

SecureObs brings together movements, presentation, food and fluid, NEWS2, medication, incidents and outstanding tasks so nurses do not have to build handover from memory.

  • Review every patient summary before signing.
  • Add clinical context where the record needs interpretation.
  • Escalate urgent risks verbally, not just in the written handover.

Care plans and patient voice

Nurses can record patient notes, create structured care plans, review patient voice entries and control what is appropriate for family/shared-care visibility.

Patient voice and family contributions must be reviewed before they influence the clinical record.
