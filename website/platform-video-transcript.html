The complete clinical operations platform for secure care.

Meet SecureObs. The complete clinical operations platform for secure care. Bringing the daily work of your care environment together, so your people can focus on what matters most: delivering safer, better evidenced care.

One connected view of care.

Clinical observations, patient voice, family sharing, care planning, medicines, staff cover, safety escalation and analytics. SecureObs connects them in one intelligent workflow, giving your team a clearer view of what needs attention.

Tap in. Start with confidence.

With NFC staff sign-in on compatible tablets, your team can tap their staff card to get started. Authorised site and ward access helps staff begin in the right place. PIN sign-in is available too.

A connection drops. Your work stays.

When a connection drops, supported entries are retained on the device in a visible upload queue. SecureObs retries when the server connection returns, so staff can see what is waiting and what has been synchronised.

Every observation. A clearer picture.

Bring general observations, enhanced observation and TESO workflows together with NEWS2 recording. See what is due, record what happened, and capture the reasons for missed checks, creating a clearer picture of care over time.

Care built around the person.

Give patient priorities a place in everyday care. Connect patient voice, notes, goals and care plans, helping teams understand the person behind the record and carry agreed care forward from one shift to the next.

Keep families meaningfully involved.

Bring families into the conversation through consent-controlled shared care. Share approved information and receive contributions for staff review, helping families stay involved while keeping clinical access carefully controlled.

Medicines, clearly recorded.

Connect authorised prescribing, medication administration and history. Record actual outcomes, refusals and omissions in the same workflow, with staff identity and timing supporting a clearer, more accountable medication record.

The right cover. A stronger handover.

Bring staff rosters, ward cover and shift handover into view. Support permanent and temporary staff access, understand who is allocated, and help the next team pick up with the information they need.

Spot what needs attention.

Connect safety escalation, incident management and scheduled security checks. Help your team see outstanding work, record actions and keep the evidence together, supporting a coordinated response across the care environment.

Turn daily activity into useful insight.

Bring operational analytics and governance evidence closer to daily practice. Review activity, recognise patterns and identify areas that need attention, with records that help managers explain what happened and how the team responded.

Your service. Your workspace.

Build an application around your company. Choose the modules your service needs, and keep the workspace focused by switching off features you do not use. Add capabilities as your organisation grows and your needs change.

Choose your starting point.

Start with Essential for core care workflows. Choose Professional for connected clinical and workforce tools. Enterprise supports larger, multi-site providers, while Hospital offers a tailored approach for hospitals and secure services.

Add the tools that matter to you.

Extend Essential with medication and eMAR, rostering, security checks, analytics, or CQC evidence and governance. Select the combination for your wards, or choose Professional with these modules included. Build for today, with room for tomorrow.

Supported from setup to everyday use.

We can support your rollout with tablet rental, full staff training, and on-site setup and deployment. From configuring your service to helping staff get comfortable, SecureObs can help you take the next step with confidence.
